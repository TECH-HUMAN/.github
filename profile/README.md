# TECH HUMAN

Technology with human purpose.

Tech Human is an investor in Trustyu.ai and needyu.ai. We help companies connect business,
human leadership, AI adoption, people and organizational governance. Each company has its own brand and commercial entry.

Trustyu.ai is a B2B accelerator of AI Native solutions, working through co-building and direct
engagements. NEEDYU V2 is developed through Trustyu and remains in pre-launch, helping people
remember, decide, act and resolve. Investment does not state ownership percentages or control.

## Companies and Products

- [Trustyu.ai](https://www.trustyu.ai/) — B2B solution accelerator; Trustyu Lens evaluates the problem, Score assesses systems, Forge provides the engineering harness, and AI Works is the development and modernization platform. Each product has its own readiness gates.
- [NEEDYU](https://needyu.ai/) — independent product invested in by Tech Human; V2 developed through Trustyu, in pre-launch.
- BMAI assesses organizational maturity. Trustyu Score assesses a concrete system, including legacy and AI-built software; their scores are separate.
